Composite Extension

Four automatic protections.

Billboard and AdOcean ads · All websites

Hides marked Billboard slots, their ad wrappers, AdOcean banners and bottom overlays.

Google ads · All websites

Blocks common Google ad servers and removes AdSense banners and marked Google ad containers, including sticky bottom ads.

Yahoo Mail

Hides the Taboola ad wrapper and the right sidebar, including its Calendar, Starred and Contacts shortcuts.

Pirate Bay

Blocks navigation to ultimatesurferprotector.com and its subdomains when initiated by piratebay.party.

Refresh existing site tabs after installing or reloading. Manage this extension at chrome://extensions.
